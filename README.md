# Parkle

An original parking-jam color puzzle game. Tap cars to drive them out of the lot, then fill each ride with riders of the matching color.

**Play:** https://jt-coder321.github.io/parkle-game/

On Android, open the link in Chrome, then tap the menu and choose **Add to Home screen** to install it. After the first visit it also works offline.

Progress saves in your own browser on your own device. The weekly race leaderboard and cloud backup are only in the Claude-hosted version.

All art, levels and music are original, and the music is synthesized live in the browser.
